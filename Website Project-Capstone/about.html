About the Underground

A hidden chocolate shop beneath a city ruled by steampunk bulldogs and powered by brass, steam, and secrets.

Our Story

The Freepaw Underground began as a small gathering place for humans looking for somewhere away from the crowded streets above.

What started as a hidden table beneath the city eventually became a full underground chocolate shop. Today, guests come through secret entrances to enjoy desserts, drinks, and conversation away from the noise of the surface.

We believe good food brings people together. Around here, chocolate is more than dessert. It is a reason to sit down, share a table, and enjoy a little freedom.

Hidden Location

Our dining room is tucked beneath the city streets, away from the steam-powered traffic above.

Fresh Every Day

Chocolate desserts and drinks are prepared fresh for every underground service.

Keep the Secret

Enjoy your visit, respect the underground, and remember that some places are better kept secret.
